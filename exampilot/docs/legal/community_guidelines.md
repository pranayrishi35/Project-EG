# Community Guidelines — Jishnu Doubt Board

_Last updated: 31 July 2026_

The Jishnu Doubt Board is a public, moderated space where defense-exam aspirants (AFCAT, NDA, CDS) ask questions and help each other. It works best when everyone keeps it respectful, useful, and safe. These guidelines are part of, and consistent with, our [Acceptable Usage Policy](/aup).

## What this space is for

- Asking genuine doubts about exam topics, syllabus, and preparation strategy.
- Answering others' questions clearly and accurately.
- Sharing study approaches and encouragement.

Every question also receives a **suggested answer drafted by Tejas, our AI study wingman.** These drafts are clearly labelled as **AI-generated**. They are a helpful starting point, not a guaranteed-correct authority — always cross-check important facts, and a human answer from the community may be more complete.

## The rules

**1. Be respectful.** No harassment, personal attacks, hate speech, slurs, or abusive language of any kind. This is enforced automatically on submission and by human moderators.

**2. Keep it public and on-topic.** This board is for exam help. There is **no private messaging** and no way to privately contact another user through this feature — by design, for everyone's safety. Do not attempt to move conversations off-platform or share personal contact details.

**3. Protect your privacy and others'.** Do not post phone numbers, addresses, email IDs, exam roll numbers, or any personal information — yours or anyone else's. You are identified by a chosen **username**, not your legal name.

**4. No spam or flooding.** Don't post the same thing repeatedly, advertise, or bulk-post to game visibility. Posting is rate-limited.

**5. No cheating or leaked-material requests.** Do not ask for or share live exam papers, answer keys to ongoing exams, or any material that violates exam integrity.

**6. No unsafe content.** No sexual content, violence, threats, self-harm encouragement, or anything illegal.

## Reporting

Every question and answer has a **Report** button. If you see something that breaks these guidelines, report it — reports go directly to a human moderation queue that our admins review. Reporting is confidential.

If you or someone else may be in danger or crisis, please contact local emergency services or a helpline immediately — the Doubt Board is not a substitute for real help.

## What happens when a rule is broken

- Content that trips our automated filter is blocked before it's posted.
- Reported content is reviewed by a human moderator and may be **hidden or removed.**
- Repeated or serious violations may result in losing access to the Doubt Board or your account, per the [Acceptable Usage Policy](/aup).

## A note for younger users

Some members of our community are minors. Everyone shares responsibility for keeping this space safe. Treat every interaction as fully public and permanent, and never share personal information.
